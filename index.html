I'm a fifth-year Computer Science PhD student at Johns Hopkins University, advised by Anqi (Angie) Liu and mentored by Daniel Khashabi. I'm currently a MATS 11.0 fellow in Berkeley, working with the OpenAI Safety Team on natural activation steering in reasoning and looped language models.

My research aims to achieve efficient and reliable adaptation in AI models. I have 3+ years of industry experience at Qualcomm, Adobe, Microsoft and Siemens, most recently enabling dynamic computation in looped LMs at Qualcomm AI Research. I received my B.Tech. in Computer Science from IIT Mandi.
